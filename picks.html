A shortlist of things worth owning, on Amazon. Cards and card supplies are the day job — the rest is everything else people ask me about.

Every link opens an Amazon search rather than one frozen product page, and that is deliberate: a hand-picked list goes out of date the week something is restocked, repriced or replaced, while a search always shows what is actually current, in stock, and at today's price.
