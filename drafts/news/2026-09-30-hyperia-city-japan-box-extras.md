# Hyperia City in Japan: a box promo and two ink drop counters you won't find in English boxes

**Summary.** Japanese Hyperia City booster boxes come with a Minnie Mouse promo
numbered 17/P4 and one of two ink drop counter cards. The English buy-a-box
Minnie is a different printing, numbered 4/RPH.

## The box promo

Takara Tomy's booster box includes **Minnie Mouse - Urban Visionary**, printed
`17/P4 · JA · 14`. It is a Promo Set 4 card, in Japanese.

English-language stores have their own version of the same card as the
buy-a-box promo, and it carries a different number: `4/RPH`, from the
Ravensburger Play Hub Promos set. So there are two printings to track, and a
collector going by the number alone should check the suffix: P4 is the Japanese
box card, RPH is the English one.

Both are on packs.ink. The Japanese one is tagged "Japanese Exclusive" and has
no TCGplayer price, because TCGplayer does not list it.

## The ink drop counters

Each Japanese box also carries one of two **ink drop counter cards**, one with
Baymax on the back and one with Merlin. The front reads: "You may remove this
counter to pay 1 ink. (This counter doesn't count as a card.)" They are printed
`JA · 14` with no collector number, so they are not part of the set's numbering.
The art is by Matthew Eng.

We file them under Extras & Oddities for Hyperia City. Only the fronts are
pictured so far.

## Dates

Hyperia City's English prerelease weekend is October 16 to 18, it reaches local
game stores on October 16 and wide retail on October 23.

## Sources and what is not confirmed

- The numbers (`17/P4`, `4/RPH`, `JA · 14`) are read off the cards.
- The box contents come from Takara Tomy's own box-bonus graphic.
- **Not confirmed: whether the Japanese counters are foil.** English packs carry
  the same two designs in the marketing slot, and Ravensburger has said there
  are no foil ink drops yet. Takara Tomy's renders show a sparkle texture the
  English ones lack. No official page says "foil", so this article does not.
- The Japanese release date is not in our records and should be added before
  publishing.
