# Hyperia City: the official gallery now shows 216 cards, including the first Epics and Enchanteds

**Summary.** The official card gallery now lists 216 cards for Hyperia City, up
from 111 a few days ago. The new entries include seven Epics and three
Enchanteds.

## What changed

The official gallery's count for Hyperia City (its "EN 14" set) more than
doubled. Ten of the newly listed cards were not yet on packs.ink, and they are
now added with official art:

- **Epic:** Nick Wilde - Providing Backup (#205), Dante - Enthusiastic Stray
  (#208), Go Go Tomago - Extreme Tester (#211), Meilin Lee - Ecstatic Fan
  (#214), Though I Have to Say Goodbye (#216), Priscilla - Efficient Clerk
  (#219), Flash - Efficient Clerk (#222).
- **Enchanted:** Un Poco Loco (#228), Max Goof - Karaoke Star (#229), Raya -
  Determined Explorer (#240).

## Dates

Hyperia City's English prerelease weekend is October 16 to 18, it reaches local
game stores on October 16 and wide retail on October 23.

## Sources and what is not confirmed

- Names, numbers, rarities, inks and costs come from the official gallery
  (cards.disneylorcana.com), as it read on 2026-10-04.
- **Not confirmed:** the final size of the set. The gallery count is still
  growing and this draft does not claim a total.
- Two cards in this set only have placeholder images on duels.ink so far, so
  they are not covered here.
