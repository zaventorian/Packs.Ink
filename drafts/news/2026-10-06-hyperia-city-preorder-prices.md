# Hyperia City singles are listed on TCGplayer, and the first prices are in

**Summary.** TCGplayer now lists every Hyperia City card, and packs.ink has matched each
one to its listing. The 204 regular non-foil cards show a TCGplayer Low price as of
October 5; foils, Epics, Enchanteds and Iconics have no price yet.

## What changed

All 242 Hyperia City cards on packs.ink are now linked to their TCGplayer product, so
each card page has a working buy link. Prices arrive with the daily update.

So far TCGplayer publishes a Low price for the 204 numbered non-foil cards (#1-#204)
and no NM Market price for any of them. That is expected before release: Market is
built from completed sales, and pre-order sales are only starting.

The highest Lows are on the Legendaries. Aurora - Delightful Musician, Max Goof -
Karaoke Star and Shere Khan - Khan Industries CEO each read $50.00; Baymax - Amped
Up, Ariel - Collector of Oddities and Tiana - Party Hostess read $40.00.

## Dates

Prerelease weekend is October 16 to 18. Hyperia City reaches local game stores on
October 16 and wide retail on October 23.

## Sources and what is not confirmed

- The listings and Low prices are TCGplayer's, through the daily TCGCSV feed, dated
  2026-10-05.
- A pre-order Low is the asking side of a market that has not traded yet. Expect it to
  move a lot before and after release; it is not a sale price.
- No prices are given for foils or chase cards because TCGplayer publishes none yet.
