# Hyperia City sealed pre-order prices: booster box Low is $205, pack $14.95

**Summary.** TCGplayer now shows Low and NM Market prices for Hyperia City sealed product, a week before local game stores get the set on October 16. The booster box Low reads $205.00 and the booster pack Low $14.95, as of October 7.

## What changed

The sealed Hyperia City listings on packs.ink now carry TCGplayer prices. The ones that matter most to players, as of the October 7 update:

| Product | Low | NM Market |
|---|---|---|
| Booster Pack | $14.95 | $15.27 |
| Booster Box | $205.00 | $215.56 |
| Booster Box Case | $799.00 | $826.66 |
| Illumineer's Trove | $111.90 | $103.45 |
| Gift Box - Beast | $69.56 | $69.77 |
| 2-Player Starter Set | $68.51 | $68.69 |

Two small counter listings also appeared in the same TCGplayer group this week: Ink Drop and Ink Drop (Blue). TCGplayer's own text marks them as counters that do not count as cards.

Single-card prices are unchanged from the October 6 update: regular non-foil cards have a Low, and none has an NM Market price yet.

## Dates

Prerelease weekend is October 16 to 18. Hyperia City reaches local game stores on October 16 and wide retail on October 23.

## Sources and what is not confirmed

- Prices are TCGplayer's, through the daily TCGCSV feed, dated 2026-10-07. Sealed NM Market figures already exist for these products because pre-orders have started trading; they will move.
- Whether the two Ink Drop listings are sold on their own or only come inside other products is not stated in the feed. Not confirmed.
- No comparison to earlier sets' launch prices is made here; that has not been checked for this draft.
