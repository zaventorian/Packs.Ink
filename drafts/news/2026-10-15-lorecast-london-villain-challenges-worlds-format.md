# Lorecast Oct 15: London's Villain Challenges, Ink Pair Prizing, a $350 Premium and $800 VIP package, Melbourne moves

**Summary.** The October 15 Lorecast detailed DLC London's two Villain Challenges and side events, announced a test of Ink Pair Prizing at the London and Tampa Main Events, and priced a Premium ($350) and a VIP ($800) Challenge package. It also moved DLC Melbourne to March 5 to 7, 2027 and set out the World Championship's format, including the Super Sealed rules.

*(Draft: slides from this stream may still be coming. Fold them in before publishing.)*

## DLC London: two Villain Challenges

London runs November 13 to 15. On top of the Main Events it now has two one-day Villain Challenges, each Core Constructed with 2,400 players and 6 rounds of Swiss.

| | Villain Challenge: Gaston | Villain Challenge: Ursula |
|---|---|---|
| Day | Saturday, November 14 | Sunday, November 15 |
| Players | 2,400 | 2,400 |
| Format | Core Constructed, 6 rounds of Swiss | Core Constructed, 6 rounds of Swiss |
| 5 or more match wins | Non-foil Gaston - Superior Archer | Non-foil Ursula - Deceiver |
| 6 match wins | Gaston - Superior Archer playmat | Ursula - Deceiver playmat |

Prizing is the same on both days:

- Every player receives a Seasonal Promo Card in Round 2 of the event.
- Each match win is worth 3,000 prize tickets, each match loss 1,000.

Both prize cards are names that already exist as booster cards: Gaston - Superior Archer is #15 in Attack of the Vine!, and Ursula - Deceiver is in Into the Inklands and Fabled. The prizes here are the promo versions.

### London side events

- **On demand:** Format Coconut, Pack Rush, Booster Draft (4 Hyperia City booster packs).
- **Scheduled:** Core Constructed, Infinity Constructed, Super Sealed, Full Box Sealed, Team Up Trios, and a Madam Mim - Resourceful Trickster Pin Event.

## Ink Pair Prizing at London and Tampa

Challenge London and Challenge Tampa will test Ink Pair Prizing at each Main Event.

- It applies to the Friday Main Event and the Saturday Main Event.
- The highest final placement for each ink pairing wins.
- That is 30 potential winners: 15 ink pairs for each of the two Main Events.
- Single-ink decks are not eligible for this prize.
- The prize is a non-foil Mickey Mouse - Brave Little Prince promo card.

## DLC Melbourne moves to March 5 to 7, 2027

After consulting Modern Brands and TAK Games, Ravensburger changed both the date and the venue of the Melbourne Challenge. It now takes place March 5 to 7, 2027 at Pullman Melbourne Albert Park. The earlier schedule had February 26 to 28.

- Special guest: Steve Warner, co-creator of Disney Lorcana TCG, in his first appearance in the Oceania region.
- Youth Division events will be held at DLC Melbourne 2027.

## World Championship: three formats, three days

Infinity Constructed, Core Constructed and Super Sealed are all used at the World Championship, December 4 to 6 at Aulani in Hawaii.

| Day | Structure |
|---|---|
| Friday | 3 rounds of Swiss in Super Sealed, then 4 rounds of Swiss in Core Constructed |
| Saturday | 4 rounds of Swiss in Infinity Constructed, then a Top 8 single elimination in Core Constructed |
| Sunday (Grand Finals) | Best-of-three matches: Match 1 Super Sealed (Bo3), Match 2 Core Constructed (Bo3), Match 3 if necessary Infinity Constructed (Bo3) |

The slide shows Minnie Mouse - Amethyst Champion, the World Championship 2026 promo (LCP C2 #20, already on the site).

### How Super Sealed works

- Players are given 6 packs each of Set 12 (Wilds Unknown), Set 13 (Attack of the Vine!) and Set 14 (Hyperia City) on site.
- They have 60 minutes to build a 50-card deck restricted to three ink colors.
- Dual-ink cards are allowed only within the three chosen colors. With Amber, Emerald and Steel, an Amber/Emerald dual-ink card is legal and an Amber/Ruby one is not.
- Players may sideboard between games in a match, including changing the ink colors, with no limit on the number of colors they change between games, as long as the deck still follows the original deck-building restrictions.
- After each round, and before the next match starts, players must go back to the deck they originally submitted.
