# Hyperia City: the official gallery now lists 242 cards, with two Iconics

**Summary.** The official card gallery now shows 242 cards for Hyperia City, up
from 216 yesterday. It now carries 18 Epics, 18 Enchanteds and two Iconics,
Mickey Mouse - Best in Town and Cinderella - Unintentional Icon.

## What changed

The gallery added 26 cards overnight, and they are on packs.ink with official
art. The chase cards now listed are:

- **Iconic:** Mickey Mouse - Best in Town (#241, Amber), Cinderella -
  Unintentional Icon (#242, Sapphire).
- **Enchanted (#223-#240), three per ink:** Aurora - Delightful Musician,
  Miguel Rivera - Street Musician, Remember Me (Amber); Rapunzel - Outgoing
  Artist, Merlin - Profoundly Curious, Un Poco Loco (Amethyst); Max Goof -
  Karaoke Star, Belle - Exceptional Writer, Shere Khan - Ruthless Entrepreneur
  (Emerald); Héctor Rivera - Worldwide Sensation, Pepita - Imelda's Right Hand,
  Mulan - Martial Arts Master (Ruby); Thomas O'Malley - Savvy Vagabond, Ariel -
  Collector of Oddities, Baymax - Amped Up (Sapphire); Marie - Caught in the
  Act, Tiana - Party Hostess, Raya - Determined Explorer (Steel).
- **Epic (#205-#222), three per ink**, including Judy Hopps - Always Vigilant,
  Tinker Bell - Curious Fairy, Belle - Reflective Writer, Daisy Duck - Savvy
  Investor and Tiana - Restauranteur.

## Dates

Hyperia City's English prerelease weekend is October 16 to 18. It reaches local
game stores on October 16 and wide retail on October 23.

## Sources and what is not confirmed

- Names, numbers, rarities and inks come from the official gallery
  (cards.disneylorcana.com), as it read on 2026-10-05.
- **Not confirmed:** that 242 is the final count. Every ink now has three Epics
  and three Enchanteds, which looks complete, but the gallery has not said so
  and this draft does not claim a total.
- No prices are quoted: none of these cards has a TCGplayer market price yet.
