This page covers what data Packs.Ink stores about you, how it's handled, and the terms you use the site under. The site is fully browseable without an account; only signed-in features collect anything.

What we collect

  • If you sign in with Google: your Google email, profile display name, and (optionally) profile photo URL. We never see your Google password.
  • If you use Collection / Decks features: the cards, sealed products, graded copies, decks (and each deck's recent edit history), follows, and favorites you save. All scoped to your account.
  • If you use watchlists or price alerts: the cards you watch, the alert rules you set, and a record of each time a rule fired.
  • Deck views: when you're signed in and open someone else's deck, we record that your account viewed it — once per deck — so creators can see a view count. Only totals are ever shown; nobody sees who viewed.
  • If you send feedback: your message, the page you were on (with any share tokens removed), your browser's user-agent string and, if you're signed in, your account email so we can reply.
  • If you use the card scanner: a photo of each scan, plus what the scanner guessed and any correction you made. Optional — see Card scanner below.
  • Error telemetry (Sentry): if the site crashes in your browser, we receive the error and stack trace, the page URL with share tokens removed, and your browser and OS type. If you're signed in, your account ID and display name are attached so we can reproduce the bug — never your email. The report doesn't include your IP address, and it doesn't include your deck lists, collection, or page contents.
  • Affiliate clicks (TCGplayer Impact): when you click a TCGplayer "Buy" link, Impact records an anonymous referral so we can earn a commission. We don't see who clicked.

What we don't collect

No advertising trackers, no fingerprinting, no cross-site tracking, and we never sell data. The site uses one privacy-focused analytics service, Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies or cross-site tracking; it sees your IP address and browser type in transit, as any web host does, and stores no personal identifiers. Browsing needs no account, and nothing in the list above is recorded until you sign in and use that feature.

Where it's stored

Account and collection data live in a Supabase Postgres database (US-based). Access is gated by Postgres Row-Level Security so only you can read or modify your own data. Your browser also keeps a copy of the card catalog and, when you're signed in, of your own collection, decks and watchlists (in localStorage and IndexedDB) so the app works offline. Clearing site data removes it; on a shared device, sign out and clear site data.

Services your data passes through

We don't sell, rent, or trade your data. It passes through these services, each only for the purpose named:

  • Supabase — database and sign-in (US).
  • Cloudflare — serves the site and its image proxy, and runs the cookieless page-view analytics described above.
  • Google — sign-in, and the web fonts on every page (Google receives your IP address and browser type when the fonts load). YouTube, when a deck page embeds a video: the player is loaded in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), so no YouTube cookie is set unless you play the video.
  • Sentry — crash reports, as described above.
  • Impact / TCGplayer — affiliate clicks.
  • api.zippopotam.us — turns the ZIP or postal code you type into "Upcoming near me" into coordinates. Only the code is sent, never your account.
  • Image hosts — card art is served through our own proxy, so Lorcast and TCGplayer don't see you. Graded-sale photos load directly from eBay's image servers and event images from Ravensburger's, which see your IP address like any image host.

QR codes on deck posters are drawn in your browser — the share link never leaves it.

Public information

Deck lists, collection shares and creator profiles are shown to other people only when you explicitly mark a deck or collection as Public, or share an Unlisted link. Your display name and avatar are visible to anyone who can see one of your decks or collections.

Tournament and Elo data. The Tournaments section, the Chicagoland Elo leaderboard and the store activity tables show player names exactly as published in public event results on Ravensburger Play or submitted by event organizers. This is public event information, not account data. If you'd like your name removed or abbreviated, contact us.

How to delete your data

You can delete individual decks, collection items, watchlists and alerts from inside the app at any time. To delete your entire account and all associated data — including every card-scanner photo you've sent — DM @HighScoreZAP on X with the email address on file. We will wipe everything within 7 days and confirm by reply.

To delete just your scanner photos while keeping your account, ask the same way and say so.

Card scanner (beta)

Identification runs entirely on your device. Your browser downloads a card index once, and the camera is matched against it locally. No photo is ever sent anywhere to be identified — the scanner works offline.

While the scanner is in beta, we also upload a photo of each scan so we can find the cards it misreads and fix them. Specifically:

  • What: the whole camera frame at the moment you tap, not a crop of the card — so whatever else is in shot is included. Alongside it we store which card the scanner guessed, why (its internal confidence and the text it read), and any correction you make.
  • Why: to develop and improve the scanner. Nothing else.
  • Who can see it: only us. Photos go to a private storage bucket, linked to your account, readable only by you and by us. They are never sold, shared, published, or shown to other users.
  • What we never do: no face detection, face recognition, or any other biometric analysis is run on scan photos, ever. No location data is attached — photos are captured straight from the camera stream, which carries no EXIF metadata, so there is no GPS tag, device serial, or timestamp beyond the upload time.
  • How long: deleted after 12 months, automatically.

It's optional. The scanner has an "Upload my scans" switch — on the notice you see the first time you open it, and in the scanner's review screen afterwards. Turn it off and the scanner still works identically; nothing leaves your phone. You can also ask us to delete photos you've already sent at any time (see deleting your data) without giving up the feature.

We record that you were shown this notice and accepted it — your account ID, the version of the notice, and the timestamp — so we can show you an updated version if what we do with these photos ever changes.
