Your overlay URL

The URL carries every setting above — paste it into OBS once and it's set. Prices refresh themselves every 30 minutes while the source is live.

Add it to OBS

  1. In OBS: Sources → + → Browser.
  2. Paste the URL above. Set Width 1920, Height 64 (any height works — the bar scales to fit; taller = bigger text).
  3. Drag it to the bottom of your scene. Done — it scrolls and refreshes on its own.
Works in Streamlabs and any browser-source overlay the same way. Tip: turn on "Transparent bar" above if you want your gameplay showing through behind the text.

Where the numbers come from

Same data as the Packs.Ink Screener: TCGplayer prices for every Lorcana card, updated daily. % change is over the window you pick, ranked biggest first. "NM Market" is TCGplayer's Near-Mint market price; "Low" is the lowest active listing in any condition.
