What's in the reel

Each frame gets its own section in the reel, introduced by a "1D Risers"-style header.
Chase rarities (Enchanted / Epic / Iconic / Promo) only exist one way, so they ignore this — same rule as the Screener.
Low is TCGplayer's published Low Price — it tracks the cheap end of the market. NM Market is their Near-Mint market price, a smoothed average of recent sales.
A price floor keeps 10¢ cards' +300% "moves" out of the bar.

Graded slabs

From our own eBay sale record, not TCGplayer — a slab is a different asset from the card. Graded movers ranks by % move; Top Sales ranks by the biggest individual sales in the window.
Graded needs a longer window than raw does: a slab sells rarely, so 1D and 1W have far fewer cards to draw on than 1M or 1Y.

Look & motion

Your overlay URL

The URL carries every setting above — paste it into OBS once and it's set. Prices update once a day (~3:30 PM Chicago); the bar re-checks once daily at 5:00 PM Chicago, so a long stream picks the new numbers up automatically.

Add it to OBS

  1. In OBS: Sources → + → Browser.
  2. Paste the URL above. Set Width 1920, Height 80 (any height works — the bar scales to fit; taller = bigger text).
  3. Drag it to the bottom of your scene. Done — it scrolls and refreshes on its own.
Works in Streamlabs and any browser-source overlay the same way. Tip: turn on "Transparent bar" above if you want your gameplay showing through behind the text.

Where the numbers come from

Same data as the Packs.Ink Screener: TCGplayer prices for every Lorcana card, updated daily. The reel cycles through your selected time frames, and within each, your rarity groups — every section ranked biggest % move first. "NM Market" is TCGplayer's Near-Mint market price; "Low" is TCGplayer's published Low Price.
