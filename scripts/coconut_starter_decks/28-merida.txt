LEADER: merida-wisp-conjurer
INKS: Amber, Amethyst, Steel
NAME: Wisp Season
DESC: Once a turn Merida lets a character enter exerted for free, and every character that enters exerted is 1 ink for 1 lore. So the deck is built from things that arrive tired anyway: nineteen Bodyguards from two to six cost, Sleepy, Flash and Bolt, who always do, and Hamish, Hubert & Harris, Lord Macintosh and Expedition Leader Mickey, who may. Powhatan's Staff buys a second exerted drop in a turn. The four Meridas pay twice: each enters exerted to draw a card, and the ones already in play draw again for every later exerted entry. Steel brings cheap damage to clear the way, and the Evasive pair of Tinker Bell and Mirabel closes out the lore the engine does not make.
