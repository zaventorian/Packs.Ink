LEADER: the-black-cauldron
INKS: Amber, Emerald, Ruby
NAME: Army of the Dead
DESC: Every character you lose can go under a Black Cauldron instead of lying in the discard, and 1 ink wakes a Cauldron to play any of them back at full price. So this deck wants things to die. Reckless brawlers have to pick fights, Goofy and Namaari take something with them when they go, and Sid Phillips makes the table pay for it. Then it gets paid for the trouble: Xanatos and Babyhead draw, Tadashi pulls ink drops, Mufasa flips a free character off the deck, Prince Eric banishes another. The Headless Horseman and Hades are the best things to bring back, since each return is a fresh when-you-play.
